# 0009. Bots v0: enemy pilots, waves, ships that take damage

- Status: Accepted with the amendment below (2026-10-03, by the developer)
- Date: 2026-10-02

## Context

- The developer (2026-10-01, late evening): "add some enemy bots to the
  game who can also fly and shoot".
- The roadmap's M1.3 ends with "a simple attack bot"; M1.5 (the skirmish
  loop) has enemy fleets and rising tiers; the vision's third control
  layer hands a ship to a bot.
- ADR 0001: the sim is plain data in fixed pools; ADR 0003: the server has
  authority; ADR 0008: everything that collides is a circle, and shots
  only hit rocks so far.
- `CLAUDE.md`'s layout has a `bots/` directory, still empty.

## Decision

- **Ships take damage** (`sim/world.h`). A hull has health and a shield
  (`HullClass::health`, `shield`, `shieldRegen`, `shieldDelay`). The shield
  takes a hit first and comes back after a rest. A ship without health is
  a wreck, out of play (no flying, firing, bumping or being hit) until the
  server removes or revives it (`ReviveShip`).
- **Teams.** Every ship and shot has a team (`PLAYERS` 0, `BOTS` 1). A shot
  passes through ships of its own team, so there is no friendly fire. The
  first rock or ship of another team on a shot's way takes the hit: an
  exact swept circle (`Contact`) now orders rocks and ships alike.
- **Ships bump each other.** Equal masses, pushed apart half the way each,
  with the hulls' bounce.
- **Events** gain `other` (the shooter or the other ship), and three types:
  ShieldHit, HullHit and ShipDestroyed.
- **Bots are pilots** (`bots/`, `sn::bots`). Each tick, `bots::Fly` reads
  the world and returns the controls a player would give: turn, thrust,
  fire. Same physics, no cheating.
  - **Target:** the nearest enemy, kept until it is lost or one much
    nearer shows.
  - **Attack runs:** at the target, aiming where a shot meets it
    (`bots::Lead`), slower once in range, firing when the nose is on the
    lead. Below 9 m it breaks away at a slant, then comes around again.
  - **Steering:** it veers from the first rock on its way and keeps apart
    from its own team.
  - **Skill** (`PilotSkill`): its aim strays (`aimError`, changing every
    half second or so), plus reaction time, sight and range. A pilot's
    memory is plain data with its own random seed, so a bot behaves the
    same each run.
- **The server referees a skirmish** (`server::MatchDesc`).
  - **Waves:** 2 bots, then one more each wave up to 8. A wave comes 4 s
    after the last one is gone, but only while a player is there. It
    arrives together from one side, 110-130 m away, in clear space, facing
    the player. Bots aim better wave after wave (aimError 0.12 down to
    0.03).
  - **Bot fighters** are weaker and slower than the player's ship: 3 shield
    and 4 hull against 8 and 12, 16 m/s against 40, and slow shots (35 m/s
    every 0.6 s, reaching 84 m) that can be dodged. The developer halved
    their speed, rate of fire and shot speed on 2026-10-02 (from 32 m/s;
    70 m/s every 0.3 s).
  - **Wrecks:** a bot's wreck is removed once its events are out. A
    player's wreck comes back home, whole, after `respawn` (3 s).
  - **Autopilot:** `--autopilot` (`MatchDesc::autopilot`) gives the
    players' ships to pilots too, for demos, soak and profiling runs.
- **The protocol is version 3.**
  - Welcome: the respawn delay.
  - Snapshot: the wave; per ship, its team, plus health and shield as
    shares of 255 (0 only for a wreck); per shot, its team.
  - Events: `other`.
- **The client** (`Flight`) draws every ship between snapshots: enemies as
  Kenney's craft_speederD tinted red, with red trails, pink-red flames and
  red shots.
  - **Effects for every ship** (`FlightEffects`), each with its own trails
    and shield. Plus:
    - a ship's arrival flashes a ring in its team's color;
    - a hit shield lights up toward the shot;
    - a hull hit throws sparks;
    - damaged hulls smoke (drawn with alpha blending, darkening);
    - a destroyed ship explodes into a flash, a fireball, sparks, smoke
      and dark debris.
  - **The HUD:** our shield and hull, the wave, enemies left and kills;
    "Wave N" big when a wave comes; "Destroyed, back in N s"; bars over
    damaged enemies; red marks at the screen's edge toward enemies out of
    sight; red edges after a hull hit.
  - **Camera:** rises from 40 to 54 m while enemies are within 70 m.
  - **Hint:** the controls fade after the first 10 s.
  - **Sounds:** sounds from other ships pan and fade with distance.

## Consequences

- Tests:
  - **sim:** hits on the shield, then the hull, then a wreck; no friendly
    fire; the shield's rest; ships bumping; reviving.
  - **bots:** the lead, turning and firing, no fire at the own team,
    flying around a rock, destroying a still ship.
  - **server:** waves come only with a player, are clear of rocks and
    grow; a wreck comes back home; bots attack a still player.
  - **A minute of autopilot:** it must win fights. Measured: 5 kills and
    1 loss in a minute; bots hit the still player 20 times in 20 s. Since
    the halving: 6 kills and no loss, and 4 hits of 10 shots in 20 s.
- A two-minute Windows run on autopilot reached wave 5, with 15 kills and
  2 losses.
- **Not yet:**
  - Fleets and orders (M1.4), and tiers and a win or loss (M1.5).
  - Blueprints for hulls and weapons: the bots' values are placeholders in
    `server.cpp`.
  - Collision damage.
  - A spatial grid: every shot checks every rock and live ship, every bot
    every rock, which is fine for a few dozen ships but not for M1.8's
    500.
  - Shots as events rather than in snapshots (ADR 0003): a snapshot with
    32 ships has room for one shot.
  - Lag compensation.

## Amendment (2026-10-03)

The developer, accepting this ADR: bots run by the server may use a
control path of their own when it lowers the CPU cost, for example thinking
less often than every tick or steering by simpler rules. Flying by a
player's controls stays the default, not a rule.
