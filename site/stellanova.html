Stella Nova

A war among the stars

A space war game. Build your colony, fit your ships, and push outward through an endless star map, where each ring is harder than the last.

Play in the browser

The browser build needs WebGPU: Chrome or Edge on a desktop, or Chrome on Android 12 and later.

This browser does not offer WebGPU, so the game will not start in it.

01

How it plays

The solo loop: grow a colony, send ships out, bring the loot home, go deeper.

  1. Colony

    Your colony produces metal, He-3 and chips over time, even while the game is closed. Command points, which pay for launches, come back over time too.

  2. Star map

    Around home lies an endless map of hex rings. Each ring outward is harder. Fog hides what lies far from explored space.

  3. Launch

    Pick a node next to explored space, then your fitted ships, with full tanks and loaded holds.

  4. Battle

    Give orders: tap space to fly there, tap an enemy to attack it. Turrets aim and fire on their own. Keys or the stick still steer.

  5. Loot

    Destroyed enemies drop crates. Fly over one to pick it up while your hold has room. Survivors bring their holds home; a lost ship is lost with its modules and cargo.

  6. Grow and go deeper

    Loot pays for colony upgrades, new ships and modules from blueprints, and repairs. The leaderboard ranks the deepest ring cleared.
